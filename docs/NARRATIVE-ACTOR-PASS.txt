WorldMemory 0.1.0-alpha.48 - Dialogue & Actor System

Adds a persistent per-player actor state layer to the alpha.45-47 narrative runtime.

Highlights:
- actor profiles under content/narrative/actors/
- relationship axes and actor flags
- actor-aware dialogue choice conditions
- choice/node relationship and flag actions
- persistent emotion and pose state
- best-effort NPC look-at/pose/emotion cues
- narrative_event quest signals
- persistent conversation history
- %actor:<id>:<key>% interpolation
- /actor author/debug command surface

Recommended conditional syntax:
when:
  actor-condition:
    actor: pale_basin.ash_collector
    relationship: trust
    minimum: 2

Choice action example:
relationship:
  add:
    trust: 1
actor-flags:
  reassured: true
emotion: relieved
