WorldMemory 0.1.0-alpha.51 — Multi-Character Scene Direction

Adds a staged-scene layer above dialogue and cutscenes:
- /scene authoring, validation, inspection and runtime diagnostics
- scene cast aliases and nearby multiplayer audiences
- NPC-to-NPC / actor-to-actor line beats
- blocking actor enter/move/exit/look actions
- synchronized participant camera enter/move/exit beats
- blocking dialogue and animation steps
- title/sound/command/wait scene actions
- story/cutscene integration through type: directed-scene
- automatic camera cleanup on completion, cancellation, failure and shutdown

The scene runtime composes existing NarrativeCore, NarrativeSessions,
NarrativeActors and NarrativeDirector systems instead of replacing them.
